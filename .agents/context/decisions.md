# SmartPI design decisions

Purpose: why the code is the way it is, and which ideas were already tried and rejected. State of the `dev-1.0` line (branched from `dev-0.7`). Module paths are relative to `custom_components/vtherm_smartpi/` unless they start with `smartpi/` (then `custom_components/vtherm_smartpi/smartpi/`). Check the code before relying on a statement: removed items below no longer exist in the tree.

Standing design rules (apply to any change):
- Simple and universal: one or two parameters, same for any on/off emitter, neutral start, capped. No over-engineering.
- HEAT has priority. COOL is a signed mirror (`a < 0` in COOL, `b > 0` in both modes) and may be slower or less tuned. Hysteresis overshoot in the bootstrap is expected.
- Disturbances (sun, occupants, other heaters) are detected and excluded from fits, never modelled inside them.
- No occupancy schedule and no hard-coded night hours. Quiet periods come from data (computed sun position, constant setpoint, valid data).
- Sensor health (stale, missing) is Versatile Thermostat's job. SmartPI only reacts to a missing value (hold, freeze), never judges a sensor.
- User-facing diagnostics carry plain values, no internal rule or bench names. Documentation describes the current state only.

## 1. Adopted mechanisms

| Mechanism | What it does | Why adopted | Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| IMC/SIMC gains | `Kp = 1/(abs(a) (lambda + L))`, `Ti = min(tau, 4 (lambda + L))`, `Ki = Kp/Ti` clamped to `[KI_MIN, KI_MAX]`; safe fallback when the model is unreliable | One explicit rule replaced hybrid heuristics; SIMC bounds Ti for lag-dominant rooms so load disturbances are corrected in hours, not days | `smartpi/gains.py::GainScheduler` |
| Near-band continuous taper | Kp/Ki damped smoothly close to the setpoint (`NEAR_BAND_TAPER_*`) | Replaced step factors; removes limit-cycle chatter at the band edge | `smartpi/gains.py`, `smartpi/const.py` |
| Per-tick anti-windup | Back-calculation `Tt = Ti` against the applicable limit (`max_on_percent` in actuator space), drains the integral only | End-of-cycle tracking left a steady offset with valves that hold back small moves | `smartpi/controller.py::SmartPIController.track_output_limit` |
| Bumpless gain rescale | `Ki*I` is preserved when Ki changes | Gain changes must not step the command | `algo.py`, `smartpi/controller.py` |
| Near-setpoint unwind | Within 1.5 sensor steps of the setpoint a P-only saturated output may unwind the integral | Quantised sensors otherwise leave the room stuck one step off the setpoint | `smartpi/controller.py::compute_pwm` (`NEAR_SETPOINT_UNWIND_STEPS`) |
| Integration step cap | A gap between two calculations integrates at most one cycle | A 9.9-cycle gap used to integrate in full | `algo.py::_update_time_tracking` |
| Deadband with shell hold | Inside the deadband core the proportional output is shaped, integral frozen in the deadband core (`I:FREEZE(deadband)`), held in the hysteresis shell and in DEGRADED | Avoids chatter at the edge without suppressing real demand | `smartpi/deadband.py`, `smartpi/deadband_output.py`, `smartpi/governance.py` |
| Governance regime matrix | Decides per regime whether gains and learning are frozen; a regime transition freezes gains and learning but does NOT hold the integral | Holding the integral on transitions left a steady error | `smartpi/governance.py` |
| Resume dead-time hold | After a window or power-shedding resume the integral is held for the active dead time | The room cannot answer sooner; integrating the error there winds up | `smartpi/resume_hold.py::ResumeDeadtimeHold` |
| Slope anticipation | In the stable phase, HEAT and COOL alike, the P and I errors read `T + c`, `c` = slope of the measured temperature x horizon, bounded by `ANTICIPATION_MAX_C` and faded in as the room nears the setpoint. The slope is read over a window equal to the horizon, bounded to 600-1800 s (`ANTICIPATION_MIN_WINDOW_S`, `ANTICIPATION_MAX_WINDOW_S`). Horizon: the emitter lag theta + tau_e of the response model of the mode (S when the split is assumed: the cautious end), HEAT and COOL alike; without a model the pulse-kept lag, else the longer of the active dead time and half the passive one. Deadband, guards, feed-forward and learning keep the measured temperature | The one mechanism of the approach to the setpoint, the same rule in both modes; derivative on measurement in series form. A window shorter than the horizon (a third of it, for instance) turns a sensor that moves by 0.05-0.10 C at once into a correction about 4 times the step and swings the command by tens of points; frozen bench (12 rooms, two draws): window = horizon passed every rule, safe everywhere, calmer command with 0.1 C sensors, replay of a field day with 81 % fewer large command jumps (largest 74 % fewer), at the cost of more overshoot in long-horizon rooms (median mean overshoot of slow rooms 0.109 -> 0.132 C, up to +0.1 C for a one-hour emitter without mass); window = half the horizon rejected (not enough on the replay); the owner adopted without a further bench the horizon capped at 30 min: it keeps the replay result below 30 min and stays near the half-horizon window (neutral in the bench) for one-hour horizons. Cuts the overshoot of slow emitters by a third or more; fast emitters equal or better. Known cost, accepted by the owner: in light-mass rooms with a very slow emitter the room spends more time slightly off the setpoint after a large step | `smartpi/anticipation.py`, `algo.py` |
| Guard cut and kick | HEAT only: output forced to 0 when measured T > SP + near_band_above and device active; released 1.5 sensor steps lower; learning, filter and dead-time estimator keep running during the cut | Limits slow overshoot; both cut and kick help on clean sensors; the reset margin kills ~1370 to ~60 bursts/day on flickering sensors | `smartpi/guards.py::SmartPIGuards`, `algo.py` |
| Guard-cut integral unwind | During the cut the integral unwinds by back-calculation (`I -= v dt / Kp`), only while the request is positive, and never below `I_floor = Kp (near_band_above - hysteresis)/Ki` | Without it a room whose integral exceeds the heat need cycles for hours; the floor keeps the real comfort-period integral after a setback | `algo.py::_unwind_integral_during_guard_cut` |
| Feed-forward FF1 | `u_ff = k_ff (T_sp - T_ext)`, `k_ff = b/a`, warm-up ramp (persisted across restarts) | Structural holding power; restoring the integral without FF caused a dip then overshoot | `smartpi/feedforward.py` |
| Applied-command receipts | SmartPI reads the Versatile Thermostat applied-command receipts for the applied power (dead time, emitter lag learning, room model) and tags no request | Local projection of scheduler behaviour was fragile; the applied power is what the scheduler reports | `algo.py::SmartPI.on_command_receipt` |
| Last valid outdoor value | Outdoor dropout keeps the last valid value without age limit for FF, setpoint model, near band; gains and learning stay frozen; integral held only until a first value ever arrived | A TTL variant overshot at sensor return; keeping the value cut the dropout error ~4x | `algo.py` (`_ext_control`) |
| Safety and runtime power | Handler reads VT runtime power; in VT safety mode the integral is held and the cycle is not SmartPI-owned | Do not integrate a stale temperature | `handler.py`, `algo.py` |
| Raw temperature for learning slopes | Slopes use raw samples; an EMA-filtered copy only serves dead-time lookback and the guard | The EMA made slope uncertainty guards useless | `smartpi/deadtime_estimator.py`, `smartpi/learning_window.py` |
| a/b estimation with windows | Robust OLS slope windows (ON for a, OFF for b), span guard against sensor flicker, median publication; per-channel drift sequences | Core model identification | `smartpi/ab_estimator.py`, `smartpi/learning_window.py`, `smartpi/ab_drift.py` |
| Integral balance pools | Windows without a robust slope contribute `T2-T1 = a U - b D` segments pooled until relative uncertainty <= 25 % | Coarse or slow sensors stayed in bootstrap forever | `smartpi/ab_window_balance.py` |
| Emitter lag and delivered power | `a` is learned after the transient with delivered power; passive `b` gate waits after a heater cut (both from the response model) | Residual emitter heat biased `b` low by ~10 %; slow emitters biased `a` | `smartpi/power_steps.py`, `smartpi/learning_window.py` |
| Dead-time estimator | Median of the last 6 detections, filtered temperature for detection; pre-start trend baseline; never shortens on weak evidence | A single aberrant detection, or a room already warming at start, shifted windows and gains | `smartpi/deadtime_estimator.py`, `smartpi/deadtime_trend.py` |
| Bootstrap | Hysteresis between configured thresholds (guards inactive), odd measurement counts (a 5, b 5, a 4 when consistent), ends after 4 heating phases when the emitter lag cannot be measured; heating phases counted, not switches | An odd count tolerates as many outliers as the next even one; bursts of short interruptions must not end it | `smartpi/ab_estimator.py`, `smartpi/diagnostics.py` (status), `algo.py` |
| Restart bridge | A stop of up to 10 min in the same mode is bridged: learning and windows resume; missing indoor temperature holds the previous command up to 10 min | VT sends no command at stop/start, the heater keeps its state | `smartpi/restart_bridge.py::decide` |
| Persistence | State written at most every 5 min (final write on stop); past timestamps survive restarts; PI memory expires after 96 h OFF while a/b is kept | Avoid I/O storms; stale integral is harmful | `state_store.py::SmartPIStateStore`, `smartpi/timestamp_utils.py` |
| Calibration by pulses, correcting | On request only, after the learning stage: the measurement pulses of the direct start (same driver) in either start mode, own count (3), through the ordinary on/off path, with limits of their own sized from the response model and the horizon (start -0.2..+0.3 C demand, gap clamp(2 H, 1-6 h), rest S + 1200 s ending early when the predicted lowest point demand + s theta_eff reaches 0.5 C, heat clamp(2 (theta_eff + H), 2-4 h), stop takeover at 0.5 C only once one sensor step deeper, cap 0.8 C, every step <= 4 h; heat and stop rules shared with the learning-stage pulses, see the next row); ends on two agreeing readings, after 3 pulses read, or after 48 h fixed (end checked during a guard cut); a stop ends the pulse (counts) and goes on; mode change, learning reset or pause cancels. Owner's answers of 23 Nov 2026: the 0.5 C limit applies to the predicted lowest point, cap 0.8 C; `a` is corrected only past 30 % of the reading (the lag only by the response model's own calibration rule) (relative to the reading) (mechanics: `algorithm-core.md`); `b` published only (owner, 23 Nov 2026: pulse `b` +36..+48 % in a wall-mass room; switch `CALIBRATION_CORRECT_B` False). Owner fixes after the rejected lot (one-seed replays): no estimator `a` measure during a calibration; clean end (rest hands back, heat goes to its stop, correction when the pulse ends; a Kp change x error is not moved into the integral at a correction; a P change is normal action); integral held after each calibration pulse until the setpoint; the calibration reading never sets the anticipation horizon; 24 h minimum interval (refusal, no exception); fallback "skip when own recent measures agree" kept behind `CALIBRATION_SKIP_WHEN_OWN_AGREE` (off) for the bench | One driver for the bootstrap, the pulses and the calibration; the learning-stage pulse limits gave no start sample and no second pulse in slow or warm-held rooms; a weekly call on a good model changes nothing below 30 %; the horizon follows the response model | `smartpi/calibration.py`, `smartpi/learning_start.py`, `smartpi/measure_pulse.py`, `smartpi/event_reading.py` |
| Valve support | Linearisation curve, `max_on_percent` applied in actuator space, no min-activation floor, VT-filter repairs | Valve position does not depend on ON time | `smartpi/valve_curve.py`, `valve_config_check.py` |
| Emitter lag (T_ar) learning | Learns the emitter lag from morning comfort steps and a multi-day room fit on quiet periods; gains use half of it, never more aggressive than the bootstrap dead time, above 2x only when both methods agree. PWM only; off in valve mode | A too-long input halved Kp and slowed rises; half the emitter lag is the effective dead time the 3 L rule expects | `smartpi/tar_learning/`, `smartpi/quiet_periods/` |
| Room model | Kalman observer (air, wall, disturbance) plus multi-day identification; published only after a qualified heat balance; feeds FF holding power and wall extra power after a setback. PWM only | A static ratio cannot reproduce wall transients; unpublished model changes nothing | `smartpi/room_model/` |
| Shared steps of every measurement pulse | The pulses of the learning stage and of a calibration share one implementation (`smartpi/measure_pulse.py`): the heating step ends when the response is seen and stands out three sensor steps AND (the room is within `PULSE_HEAT_BACK_C` = 0.1 C of the demand side, so the stop starts high, OR the step lasted `PULSE_HEAT_EXTEND_MAX_S` = 30 min: owner, 23 Nov 2026, the fit reads `a` lower the longer the heat; rejected: no extension, <= active dead time, predicted peak, within 0.3 / 0.5 C), or 0.3 C past the setpoint, or at its time limit; the regulation takes over a stop at `PULSE_TAKEOVER_C` = 0.5 C only once the room is one sensor step lower than at the start of the stop, and in any case at the cap (`PULSE_TAKEOVER_CAP_C` = 0.8 C, the calibration's own cap for its pulses); the stop still ends on the confirmed peak. While the direct start's learning stage runs, the published sensor state, `control.phase`, `learning.stage` and `model.confidence` are `learning_pulses`, `Learning`, `measurement pulses`, `ab_learning` (internal phase enum unchanged; the bootstrap start keeps its values) | Field finding: a stop handed over at 0.5 C under after 18 min, before the peak could be confirmed (about 30 min without heating), left the dead time after a stop unmeasured and blocked the learning, since PWM cycles never leave 30 min without heating; the short heat step only warmed the room 0.2 C; nothing published may say bootstrap or hysteresis when no bootstrap runs | `smartpi/measure_pulse.py`, `smartpi/learning_start.py`, `smartpi/diagnostics.py`, `sensor.py` |
| One measurement event, one reader, starting values | A heating phase and the peak after its cut are one event (`smartpi/measure_event.py`, one peak search `smartpi/peak_search.py`) with a source (pulse, bootstrap, calibration, regulation); one reader fits it as a whole (`smartpi/event_reading.py`, `event_fit.py`, background job) and two agreeing readings (20 % on `a` and the lag) give the a/b estimator starting values once per blank start (`smartpi/starting_values.py`). Owner's rules: `a` released at the learning's first own publication, `b` when its own time constant is reliable; no history or counter moves with a starting value; the feed-forward warm-up reads the larger of a latched credit (5 per value given) and the own count. With the bootstrap start the regulation is unchanged (identical simulation traces on ten rooms); the kept lag is published only. The direct start is offered as the user option `smart_pi_learning_start` (bootstrap by default); it acts on a blank start only; the kept values are per mode | One mechanism per need: the product read a heating phase six ways and the peak three; the readings replace nothing user-visible besides the direct start | `smartpi/learning_start.py` and the modules listed. Bench: lot 1 rejected on `b` (first window ended at the peak, power as cycle fraction; both fixed); lot 2 rejected on overheat cuts in one room and draw, caused by the a-sample outlier gate (`pitfalls.md`), waived by the owner on 22 Nov 2026 |
| Early stop-peak confirmation (direct start) | Estimator's stop search also confirms on a closed bin (or the bin in progress at the next heating) >= max(0.05 C, 2 sensor steps, half the rise since the cut) under the best; 1800 s stays the ceiling. Direct-stage `b` windows: confirmed peak, or cut + 3 known lags | Owner, 23 Nov 2026 (field finding of the pulse-steps row); shorter pulses accepted. Open (stage B, bench): published reading, 3-lag gate everywhere, full power vs the power cap | `smartpi/peak_search.py`, `smartpi/deadtime_estimator.py`, `smartpi/learning_window.py` |
| Relative floor of the a/b outlier gate | 2 % of the history median under the MAD of the gate (`AB_MAD_REL_FLOOR`), so a tight early history cannot lock the learning | Diagnosis of an overheat-cut failure (10th own `a` at 194 h instead of 28 h); frozen bench (28 runs, bootstrap and direct): no lock in any run, 26 runs identical, rules not met only by a usefulness rule that took slow collections for locks; adopted by the owner on 22 November 2026, not ported to 0.6 (the lock is a direct-start case) | `smartpi/ab_estimator.py`, `smartpi/const.py` |
| Learning block (pause, disturbance, heating unavailable) | Each blocks every learning, regulation unchanged; entities (only `on` counts) read each tick, never write the pause. Disturbance: integral free. Heating unavailable: integral held, then resume hold over the dead time, no 20 min pause (owner, Nov 2026) | Secondary sources or an idle emitter bias the model; an off boiler wound the integral | `smartpi/learning_block.py` |

## 2. Removed / rejected approaches

| Approach | Why rejected or removed | Evidence | Instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setpoint reference governor (model-based post-PI command cap with handoff, reserve selectors, prediction falsification) | Complexity (~2400 production lines) for no measurable gain; the cap bound sat exactly at target on a biased model, so the room could plateau under target for hours | With it removed, switch-mode landings were practically unchanged; with valve position retention landings overshot 0.05-0.25 C more on some steps, but the cap was worse on as many other rows | Late-braking P trajectory only; the PI output is never capped near the target |
| Direct command cap (cap enforced after soft/timing constraints, P on raw target) | Raw-target P plus legacy trajectory diverged 13-56 min before any cap acted | Cap-enforcing arm never settled and had worse IAE than the same arm without the cap; mirror HEAT/COOL effects failed | None; do not cap the post-PI command |
| Monotone handoff / sensor-phase variants, guard-only profile, authority-free governor | Failed effect symmetry and plateau-qualification gates in every orientation tested | Gains on one orientation, regressions or invalid primes on the others | Stop; the approach to the setpoint is the slope anticipation |
| Terminal braking release (governor-style release of the cap inside the deadband) | Never exercised in natural closed-loop runs (no retirement, no downstream effect) and jump gates failed identically in baseline | Six prime-valid cases, zero retirements | Nothing to release: there is no cap |
| HEAT setpoint landing cap (model-aware cap, residual release latches) | Superseded by the same analysis as the governor | Equal rows better and worse after removal | Slope anticipation |
| Late-braking P trajectory (P branch on a shaped setpoint near the target, options "Setpoint filter" and "Release tau factor", recovery plateau detection on its disturbance side) | Two mechanisms for the approach to the setpoint are one too many; it only shaped the P error while the command at the crossing came from the feed-forward and the integral | Without it and with the slope anticipation, fast emitters are equal or better and slow emitters overshoot a third less | Slope anticipation, P and I on the raw setpoint |
| Eris cycle-based tuning (kept as inert research on a separate branch, not in this tree) | Nominal gain no better than IMC/SIMC; robust cases mixed | Nominal IAE +0.5 to +1.3 %, mismatch cases -4.6 to -0.3 % median with worst cases up to +18 % | IMC/SIMC with the current scheduler |
| Hybrid heuristic Kp/Ki tuning, step near-band factors | Replaced by explicit rule and continuous taper | n/a | `GainScheduler` |
| SIMC Ti rejected once (overshoot on steps and window openings), then adopted | The rejected variant removed the model bias the integral carried (setback recoveries, undetected losses); adopted only together with a bounded near-setpoint unwind (1.5 steps, P-only saturation) and a stalled-recovery condition | Combined set cut IAE ~3 % with the unwind bounded | `Ti = min(tau, 4 (lambda + L))` with `NEAR_SETPOINT_UNWIND_STEPS` |
| Ki floor as the main protection | Binds in nearly all runs, so Ki was effectively constant; the floor was judged a defect for inert rooms | Floor reached in 100 % of runs on the older rule | SIMC Ti; floor stays as a lower clamp only |
| Integral guard (setpoint-change guard, directional guard with unwind, stalled-recovery release, plateau exit, soft release) | Blocking the integral during a recovery left rooms below the setpoint for hours (cold walls, shedding); each release variant traded plateau against overshoot | Release at full rate overshot 0.10-0.13 C; the soft release (0.2x rate) fixed stalls but kept state machinery; removing the guard closed the gap in about half an hour | Integral works during the whole recovery; only the resume dead-time hold, per-tick anti-windup and guard cut remain. Do not reintroduce a setpoint-change guard |
| Calibration reachability judged by the thermal twin (passive and full-power equilibria) | The model the calibration is meant to repair could veto it: a disturbance estimated under power, divided by a small `b`, gave an absurd passive equilibrium and every calibration ended at once | Field case: passive equilibrium near 28 C for an outdoor temperature near 12 C | No reachability rule: the calibration uses pulses near the setpoint, bounded by the pulse limits |
| Calibration phase machine (cool down to SP - 0.3, heat up to SP + 0.5, cool down to SP) and its pause on a stop | A second driver of the same measurement; deep excursions; needed a reachability rule and a pause machinery; fed the dead-time detector the computed command | Design study of the 1.0 redesign | Calibration by pulses |
| Automatic calibration (`AutoCalibTrigger`: stagnation snapshots, retries, `model_degraded`) | With pulses it could only add dead-time samples; dead times are reliable from the first sample and never revert, so its live triggers were `a`/`b` stagnation, which pulses cannot repair; `model_degraded` drove nothing | Design study of the 1.0 redesign (code reading) | Calibration on request only |
| Regime-transition integrator hold | Acted as an implicit anti-windup only | Removing it cut IAE ~1.3 %; shortened error after regime changes | Per-tick anti-windup; gains and learning still freeze on transitions |
| End-of-cycle anti-windup tracking | Left a steady offset with valves that hold back moves | Offset removed; overshoot slightly higher in that case (dpercent 10) | Per-tick back-calculation |
| Guard cut gated on the filtered near-band | Delayed the cut on slow overshoots | Ungated cut adopted after two-stage confirmation; cool side unchanged | Cut on measured temperature only; HEAT only |
| No guard cut / no guard kick | Both help on clean sensors | Without the cut IAE +2.4 % and many overshoot rows; without the kick +1.4 % | Keep both |
| Unwind during the cut by integrating the error toward zero without a floor | Removed the real comfort-period integral after an evening setback, slowing the next morning rise | Rise +30..+60 min on slow rooms | Floor `I_floor` |
| Static energy-balance ratio (`b/a`) for FF holding | Accurate in clean cells but a correct static ratio regulated worse than the biased online value that compensated wall transients | Several cells reached fewer steps with the right ratio | Room model that includes the wall, published only when qualified |
| Feeding the full learned T_ar to the gains | It is an emitter lag, not the effective dead time the 3 L rule expects; halved Kp and slowed rises | Rise 87 to 156 min on a lag room; half the value neutral to better | Half of it, bounded, double-method rule |
| Emitter lag learning for valves | Value only as good as the valve curve; short rises on slow emitters | Left rise short of the setpoint | Off in valve mode |
| Moments estimator and dedicated emitter lag calibration night | Too few accepted windows (zero with 0.2 C sensors, almost none on fast emitters, none on the real rooms) or too long for slow slabs | Accuracy fine when accepted, availability failed | Morning-step fit plus multi-day room fit |
| Learning-window variants: centroid-linearised delta; OFF windows anchored after the passive dead time | Gain smaller than the noise floor; regulation mixed | Median b bias only halved or equal, fewer publications | Current windows; remaining items waited for winter data |
| FF-06 asymmetric dead time on power decreases | No effect with whole-cycle prediction buffers | 115 of 120 cells bit-identical, IAE +0.7 % | Deferred to winter data |
| Outdoor value with a 2 h TTL | Overshoot when the sensor returns after expiry | Startup/return overshoot | Last valid value, no TTL |
| Alpha-per-dt EMA, EMA-slope for learning | Identical at 60 s cadence; EMA slopes biased early | Slope estimate vs truth off by up to x8 at 0.1 C/h | Raw temperature slopes |
| Slow feed-forward trim (FF2), short-horizon feed-forward (FF3), 1R1C thermal twin, command ownership and causal power trace | The trim never had meaningful authority on the command (the integral carries the model bias), FF3 never started in the owner's rooms, and the twin existed only to support FF3 and diagnostics: one mechanism per job, so the holding power (FF1) is the only feed-forward and the integral absorbs the rest. Without a trim there is nothing to attribute to a request, so SmartPI tags none and reads the applied power from the receipts | FF3 active about 0.5 % of bench evaluations; no field activation | FF1 holding power and the integral; the anticipation for the approach to the setpoint |
| Occupancy ranges, clock-defined nights | Not declared by users, not universal | n/a | Quiet periods from data |
| 2R2C in the regulation path | Not identifiable on one night, sleepers and sun bias it, sensors too coarse; also bench plants only | Free fits invent a wall in rooms without mass | 2R2C used for simulation plants and inside the room model, with a reduced 1R1C fallback |
| Early resolution of the delivered-power model on fast emitters | Not needed: fast emitters learn, they just need more heating phases | A fast electric radiator eventually learned its 8 min emitter lag | Keep current gates |
| Bootstrap exit on the heating-balance lag (balance as lag source and exit) | Later exit in slow-emitter rooms (the `a` collection restarts when a balance lag is published), benefit 12 % for 30 % required, lag 41 % too low in a fast massive room; dropped for good by the owner (2026-11-21) | Frozen screening under the grid | Exit by the intercept estimator or the full collection; the balance lag only sets the anticipation horizon |

## 4. Open questions / not yet decided

- Deferred learning items awaiting winter field data (mild-weather `b` is biased low by free gains): power returning inside an OFF window, optional stopping in sliding windows, recomputing stored `a` measurements with the current `b`, dead time bias of about one sensor period, window anchor and EMA alpha per dt.
- FF-06 per-transition variant (increase delayed by heat dead time, decrease by cool dead time) to be tested on winter dead times.
- Dedicated tuning for slow emitters (floor heating, heavy radiators): they may overshoot more after a large setpoint increase.
- Room model: first publication needs about 6 days; short setbacks with heavy walls usually keep it unpublished; valves unsupported; a disturbed-night rule misses gains present every night; sensors of 0.1 C with mass rooms are refused.
- Emitter lag learning: valve mode unsupported; very slow slabs (emitter lag of an hour) and rooms with a bootstrap dead time far below the effective one overshoot and may never publish.
- Guard-cut floor assumes the feed-forward already balances losses; cold start or slow sensors can unwind a still-needed integral.
- Computing a recommended PWM cycle in SmartPI and publishing it as a repair is a possible follow-up.
- COOL guard cut and the near-band override clamp are untouched; slow COOL and no-demand runs never block a decision.
